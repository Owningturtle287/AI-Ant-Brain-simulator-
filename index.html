    Controls

    Look around
    Drag the arena to orbit. Use Top or Side to inspect the body from that angle. Pinch, scroll, or use + and − to zoom.
    Guide the ant
    Turn on Guide ant and tap a patch of ground. You can also steer with the arrow keys while the arena has focus.
    Watch the brain
    Choose a region in the Brain tab. Bright dots show modeled activity and the connection lines show recent signals. Pause or slow down to inspect a neuron.
    Change a foraging cue
    Use Place food in the Brain tab, then tap the ground. Show trail reveals the scent, and Clear trail lets the worker search without the seeded trail.
    Inspect a step
    Pause or slow the simulation, then turn on joint markers or foot contacts in the Specimen tab.
